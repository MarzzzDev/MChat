Privacy & Credits

Last updated: October 4, 2026

Privacy

Short version: MChat has no accounts, no cookies, no analytics and no database. It does not collect or store personal data. Your settings live only in the overlay link.

What MChat stores: nothing on a server. MChat is a static website that runs in your browser or streaming software. The Twitch channel name and your settings are kept only in the overlay link you copy, so only share it with people you trust.

Twitch chat: the overlay connects to Twitch chat anonymously, without logging in, and shows the channel's public messages in your browser. MChat does not log, save or forward them.

IP addresses: your browser connects directly to the services listed below, so they receive your IP address and normal browser information, like any website you visit. MChat does not receive or control that data. This site is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may keep standard server logs.

Your rights: if you are in the EU, EEA or UK you can ask what data is held about you, ask for it to be corrected or deleted, object to its processing, and complain to your local data protection authority. Since MChat stores no personal data there is normally nothing to retrieve, but you can contact me any time.

Children: MChat is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect data from anyone.
