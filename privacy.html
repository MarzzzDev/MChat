About MChat

A free Twitch chat overlay for OBS and other streaming software.

What is MChat?

MChat is a highly customizable Twitch chat overlay. It shows your channel's chat on stream with the emotes, badges and name paints your viewers already use on 7TV, FrankerFaceZ, BetterTTV and more.

No login, no install. Type a channel name, adjust the look with the live preview, copy the link and paste it into a Browser Source. That's the whole setup.

MChat runs entirely in the browser source. It connects to Twitch chat anonymously, so it never asks for your account or a token, and it keeps up with changes mid-stream, like new 7TV emotes, without a refresh.

Features

  • Emotes: Twitch, 7TV, FrankerFaceZ and BetterTTV, global and channel. 7TV personal emote sets and zero-width emotes are supported, and 7TV emote changes show up live.
  • Effects: FFZ and BTTV emote effects such as ffzCursed, ffzSpin, ffzW and more.
  • Badges: Twitch, FFZ (including custom moderator and VIP badges), 7TV, Chatterino, Homies, BTTV, DankChat and Moltorino. Each platform can be turned on or off on its own.
  • 7TV paints: usernames use the viewer's 7TV paint or color.
  • Chat behavior: replies, /me actions, GIFs, and live removal of deleted messages, timeouts, bans and chat clears.
  • Bots: hide known bots and ! commands to keep chat clean.

Customize the look with:

Get started

  1. Open the setup page and enter your Twitch channel name.
  2. Adjust the settings and watch the live preview update.
  3. Click Copy overlay link.
  4. In OBS, add a Browser Source and paste the link as the URL.
  5. Set the source size to fit your scene. The background is transparent unless you turn it on.

Your settings are stored in the link itself, so keep the link to reuse your setup, or edit the setup page again and copy a new one.

Privacy

MChat has no accounts, cookies, analytics or database, and it does not collect or store personal data. It is a static site hosted on GitHub Pages. Your channel name and settings live only in the overlay link, so share it only with people you trust.

Your browser connects directly to the services listed above, plus jsDelivr (Twemoji images) and Google Fonts, so those services receive your IP address like any website you visit. MChat does not receive or control that data, and their own privacy policies apply. Chat messages are shown in your browser and are never logged, saved or forwarded.
